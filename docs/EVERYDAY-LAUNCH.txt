Everyday launch: scripts/update-and-start.sh

With no arguments (or --launch), this verifies the running daemon's exact,
approved source and the corresponding GUI manifest. It reuses the existing
GUI or starts that GUI. It does not fetch, build, install, restart the daemon,
change protection, replay caps, or edit autostart. Unavailable/unapproved or
modified releases refuse; they never fall through to an install.
--dry-run alone previews this launch decision without starting a GUI.

An update is explicit: scripts/update-and-start.sh --update --local
(--local skips fetching; --update --dry-run --local previews the update).
